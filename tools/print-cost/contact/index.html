To describe a problem, include your browser, the tool’s name, the steps you took, and the result you expected. Use a short example that does not include confidential information.

Before reporting a problem

Check that the material units match the package size, and remember that margin is different from markup. Choose the input basis before entering costs: per item or whole plate. Compare the estimate with your slicer output and measured electricity use.
