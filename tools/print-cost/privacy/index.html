Privacy policy

Your cost estimates stay in your browser

Cost inputs, comparison pins and customer quote previews are held in page memory. Pins are cleared when you leave or reload the page. The app does not send them to a server. Choosing Save profile stores the inputs in localStorage on this browser and device; Delete saved removes that profile. Choosing Share link creates a URL fragment containing the inputs. The fragment is not sent in the initial HTTP request, but anyone who receives the full link can read it. Shared devices and browser extensions may access locally stored information.

Hosting information

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Your browser requests website files from GitHub, and GitHub may log information such as your IP address for hosting and security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Optional Google Analytics

If you choose Allow analytics, this site loads Google Analytics to measure page visits and feature usage. Google receives page titles and paths, fixed tool labels, and technical information such as browser and device information. It may receive your IP address while handling requests. Analytics cookies identify browser sessions. Read Google’s Privacy Policy.

This integration does not send your subtitle text, filenames, clipboard contents, or cost inputs to Analytics. Query strings and URL fragments are removed from the page and referrer URLs we send. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled.

Your choice

The Google tag does not load until you allow analytics. No thanks keeps the tools available without analytics. Your choice is saved in localStorage on this device. Use Analytics settings in the footer to change it; declining stops this integration and removes its prefixed cookies. Choices apply to the tools in this directory, not other projects on this host.

Advertising and external links

This version does not include advertising code. Links to other websites follow those websites’ policies. Information you choose to share with the maintainer is subject to the privacy terms of the service you use. See contact details.
