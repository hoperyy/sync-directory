About Print Cost

Print Cost helps makers estimate the real cost of a 3D print. It makes material, electricity, machine time, hands-on labor and repeat-print risk visible, then calculates a price after selling fees for a chosen margin. Compare scenarios, allocate whole-plate costs and create a simple customer quote. All rates are provided by you.

Built and maintained by hoperyy

This is an independent project. Its source is maintained in a website workspace by hoperyy. The calculations and file transformations run in your browser. No account is required.

A focused first version

The model supports identical items entered per item or as one whole plate. It assumes full-cost, independent failures. Failed attempts repeat material, electricity and machine time; retry labor is entered separately. Successful labor, extras and one-off order setup are not repeated. Selling fees apply to the pre-tax item price, with a fixed fee once per order. It does not parse STL or G-code, optimize build plates, estimate taxes or fetch current material prices.

The tools are free today. If ads or paid services are introduced, the privacy information and product descriptions will be updated before those services launch.
